## What people mix up

**Free pickup versus free laundry.** Only the driving is free.

**Same-day drop-off versus same-day delivery.** $2.29 per pound is the rush price for a bag you bring to the store. Home delivery is usually a next-day pickup and a return within about 24 hours after that pickup. Sometimes a same-day pickup window exists. Cents is where that shows up. An article cannot promise it.

**24/7 store versus 24/7 pickup.** You can walk into the laundromat at midnight. That does not mean a driver will collect a bag at midnight, or that the phone will be answered at midnight. Forty-four washers and fifty dryers describe the store. They do not describe the size of the delivery fleet.

**Tax.** Published Wash Bodega per-pound rates include tax. Do not add 8.25 percent on a napkin and then get angry at checkout.

**Dry cleaning.** Not offered. A "free pickup" that quietly expects suits and silk is the wrong door. Those garments need a cleaner, and Wash Bodega is not one.

## A five-minute price check before you book anyone

1. Write down the per-pound rate.
2. Write down the minimum in dollars, not just in pounds.
3. Ask whether pickup and delivery are $0 or whether a fuel or service fee lands later.
4. Ask whether tax is inside the rate.
5. Ask where the clothes are washed. A street address is a better answer than a service area map.
6. Price your real weight. Twenty pounds at $1.69 is $33.80. Twenty pounds at $1.89 is $37.80. Twenty pounds at $3.95 is $79. The minimum can override all three if your bag is light.
7. If the bag is near 40 pounds, compare the giant-bag price against the per-pound total.
