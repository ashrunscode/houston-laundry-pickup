Laundry pickup and delivery Houston shoppers usually want one number. The useful number is the whole order, not the trip fee.

## Who this price fits

The $1.69 rate is for people who would rather not spend the evening at a machine.

- Families in apartments without an in-unit washer, especially around Westbury, Meyerland, Sharpstown, and the Bellaire vicinity
- Medical Center workers who can leave a bag for a driver and collect a folded stack after a shift
- Households that already drive past W Bellfort and want the option to drop the bag instead
- Anyone comparing a "free pickup" ad against a real per-pound minimum

If the load is under 15 pounds, pickup is usually the wrong product. The minimum still applies. Self-service at the same store starts at $3.25 a wash, with machine prices posted on the floor, and the customer entrance is open 24 hours. Under 15 pounds, or if you like doing it yourself, use the laundromat.

## What "free" covers

Free pickup and free delivery means Wash Bodega does not add a trip fee, a fuel surcharge, or a delivery upcharge on top of $1.69 per pound. The driver still comes. You still pay for washing, drying, and folding by the pound, or by the giant bag.

A service that advertises free pickup and then charges far more per pound can cost more than $1.69 with a 15-pound minimum. Always multiply. A 20-pound order at $1.69 is $33.80. The same 20 pounds at $2.50 would be $50 before any minimum. The word "free" does not answer the question.

## What is not in the $1.69

- Comforters and other bulky items are priced separately.
- Dry cleaning is not offered. Do not put dry-clean-only garments in the bag.
- A published ZIP code does not confirm the route. Core pickup ZIPs are 77035, 77096, 77071, 77074, and 77085. Broader coverage includes the Medical Center area, Katy, The Woodlands, and surrounding Houston neighborhoods such as Westbury, Meyerland, Sharpstown, and the Bellaire vicinity. Confirm the street address when you book.
- Store customer access is 24/7, with 44 washers and 50 dryers. That does not mean pickup, phone coverage, or wash-and-fold intake runs on a 24/7 window.
- Turnaround is typical, not guaranteed.

## Three priced examples

A 15-pound starter order, enough for a one-bedroom week of clothes and towels, is $25.35 on pickup. The same weight dropped off for next day is $19.35. Driving it in saves $6, counting both trips as your time rather than a fee.

A 30-pound family order is $50.70 on pickup. Next-day drop-off is $38.70. The $12 difference is two trips to W Bellfort.

A full 40-pound giant bag is $47 once, or $39 on the weekly 4-bag plan. The weekly plan's monthly minimum is $156. That plan makes sense when you actually send about four full bags a month. It does not make sense as a coupon for one light hamper.
