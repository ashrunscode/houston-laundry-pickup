The checklist

Use the same questions on every site, including this one.

  1. Is the rate printed? "$1.69 per pound" is a rate. "Affordable" is not.
  2. What is the minimum in dollars? Fifteen pounds at $1.69 is $25.35. A lower pound-minimum at a higher rate can still cost more.
  3. Is the trip actually $0? Free pickup and free delivery should survive until checkout. Watch for fuel, service, or "small order" fees.
  4. Is tax inside the number? Wash Bodega's published per-pound rates include tax.
  5. Who washes the clothes, and where? A storefront at 6604 W Bellfort Ave is a place you can visit if a bag goes missing. A logo with no address is a harder phone call.
  6. Whose driver is it? Wash Bodega sends its own drivers. That does not make them magically on time. It does mean the route is theirs to confirm.
  7. What does "24 hours" modify? Here it modifies store access, not delivery and not the phone.
  8. What is refused? Dry cleaning is a refusal, not a footnote. Specialty and bulky items are priced separately everywhere serious.
  9. Is the turnaround a guarantee? "Typically within 24 hours of pickup" means most orders, not a refund clause you invented.
  10. Can the household book in Spanish? Se habla español matters when the person who meets the driver is not the person who found the website.

A service that scores well on this list can still be the wrong service for a 8-pound gym bag. Best is conditional.
