Laundry Pickup Houston: How Wash Bodega Pickup Works on W Bellfort

Laundry pickup Houston, when it is this service, starts and ends at a real laundromat: Wash Bodega, 6604 W Bellfort Ave, Houston, TX 77035. You book a window, leave everyday laundry in a bag, and Wash Bodega’s own drivers take it to that store. The store washes, dries, and folds it, then a driver brings it back. The price is $1.69 per pound. The minimum is 15 pounds, or $25.35. Pickup is free and delivery is free. Tax is included. Free pickup is not free laundry. Specialty items are priced separately. The bag is typically back within 24 hours of pickup. That timing is usual, not a guarantee. The phone is (832) 834-5689. Se habla español.

What you are actually buying

You are not buying a membership in an app. You are buying wash, dry, and fold of ordinary laundry, plus two drives. The drives are priced at zero. The laundry is not. A one-bedroom week that weighs 15 pounds is $25.35. A fuller 25-pound order is $42.25. A 40-pound order at the per-pound rate is $67.60, which is the moment to look at a giant bag instead: $47 one time, $44 per bag on a two-bag month ($88 monthly minimum), or $39 per bag on a four-bag month ($156 monthly minimum). Bags hold up to 40 pounds. A half-empty bag is a bad use of the flat price.

The same building will take the bag from your hands if you would rather drive. Next-day drop-off is $1.29 per pound. Same-day drop-off is $2.29 per pound. Both keep the 15-pound minimum. Same-day service is the in-store rush price. It is not a promise that a driver will return your laundry the evening of the pickup. Most pickup requests are for the next day. A same-day pickup window sometimes exists. The booking screen is the only place that knows.

The building the clothes go to

Wash Bodega is a 24-hour laundromat in the Westbury area of Southwest Houston. Customer access has been open all day and all night since August 1, 2026. There are 44 washers and 50 dryers. Staff are on site around the clock, and that fact still does not make the phone, the wash-and-fold intake desk, or the delivery vans a 24-hour product. People confuse those on purpose in ads. This page will not.

There is no dry cleaning. A garment whose label says dry clean only should not be in the pickup bag. Comforters and other bulky pieces are priced on their own, not hidden inside the $1.69. Self-service washes start at $3.25 for people who want to stay with a small load. Machine-size prices are posted in the store. Under 15 pounds, that is usually the right door.

Step by step

  1. Check the weight in your head, then ignore it at billing. If you are clearly under 15 pounds, stop and use a machine. If you might be near 40, compare a giant bag before you check out.
  2. Open the order link from the pickup page or go directly to Cents. Enter the street address.
  3. Choose wash and fold pickup and delivery, then per pound or by the bag. If no window appears, that address is not available then. Do not invent a curb time the software refused.
  4. Write the bag’s location in boring detail. Gate code, apartment, leasing desk, side door, color of the bag. No-contact pickup fails when the note is “it’s out front.”
  5. Bag everyday washable laundry only. Empty pockets. Leave out dry-clean-only clothes and anything you need a separate quote for, including comforters, until you have that price.
  6. Be there in the sense that the bag is there. You do not have to hand it over in person if the instructions are good. You do have to put the bag where you said it would be.
  7. The driver brings it to 6604 W Bellfort Ave. Washing happens at Wash Bodega, not at a plant in another county under a different name.
  8. Folded laundry comes back, typically within 24 hours of the pickup. If a storm, a missed code, or a bag that was not outside breaks that pattern, the clock was never a guarantee.

Calling (832) 834-5689 is the right move when you want a person to confirm a bulky item or a confusing gate before you book. It is the wrong move at 1 a.m. if what you need is a driver. Store doors and phone coverage are different systems.
